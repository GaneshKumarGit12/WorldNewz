# WorldNewzs Content Strategy & Thin Content Fix

These guidelines are established to fix the "thin content" issue on `https://worldnewzs.in` and comply with Google AdSense and Google News indexing policies. Keep these requirements in mind for all content analysis, article generation, and database updates.

---

## 1. Article Length
- **Minimum Post Length**: Aim for **600–1,000 words** minimum per post.
- **Pillar/Trending Articles**: Go deeper, targeting **1,500–2,000 words** for key categories (Politics, Technology, Business, Science-Health) and evergreen topics to boost SEO and AdSense eligibility.
- **Organization**: Use subheadings (`## ` for H2, `### ` for H3) to divide content into clear, logical sections.

## 2. Depth & Value
- **Structural Coverage**: Every expanded article must cover:
  1. **Background/Context**: The historical baseline or explanation of the topic.
  2. **Current Updates**: The latest breakthroughs, facts, or real-world events.
  3. **Expert Opinions**: Quotes and commentary from qualified observers or official spokespeople.
  4. **Strategic Implications**: Geopolitical, economic, or industry effects.
  5. **Future Outlook**: Predictions and next steps.
- **Authoritative Signals**: Include statistics, numerical data, and citations of authoritative sources.
- **FAQ Section**: Add a `## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)` section at the end of each article containing at least 3 relevant questions and answers.

## 3. SEO Structure & Formatting
- **Keyword-Rich Titles**: Write catchy, SEO-friendly headlines (e.g., “Latest AI Trends in 2026: What You Need to Know”).
- **Meta Descriptions**: Write a compelling description of **150–160 characters** capturing the article's core angle.
- **Short Paragraphs**: Write in short paragraphs (2–3 sentences each) to optimize readability and user engagement on mobile devices.
- **Internal Links**: Include 1–2 internal links to related WorldNewzs categories or sections (e.g. `[Technology News](https://worldnewzs.in/technology)`).
- **External Links**: Include 1–2 external links to authoritative wire services or credible sources (e.g. `[BBC News](https://www.bbc.com)`).

## 4. Example Expansion Framework (850–900 words)
- **Introduction (100 words)**: Introduce the topic and why it matters today.
- **Background (150 words)**: Provide context or history of the topic.
- **Current Trends (200 words)**: Detail the latest updates, breaking facts, and examples.
- **Challenges & Controversy (150 words)**: Discuss limitations, ethics, or challenges.
- **Future Outlook (150 words)**: Analyze next steps, future opportunities, and predictions.
- **Conclusion & FAQs (100+ words)**: Summarize the article and provide 3 key FAQs.
