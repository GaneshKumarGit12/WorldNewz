# SkilledAgent RefinementNews Instructions

Whenever you are analyzing, generating, or managing the news refinement pipeline for WorldNewzs, you MUST follow these guidelines:

## 1. Dynamic Content Refinement
* **Title & Description Overwriting**:
  - Raw articles fetched from APIs must be refined to have a punchy editorial title and a cohesive 2-3 sentence summary.
  - In lists and feeds, overwrite the raw `Title` and `Description` with the cached refined `Headline` and `Summary` before sending them to the UI.
* **Image Refinement**:
  - Add search keywords generated by Gemini to pull high-quality featured photos from Unsplash: `https://images.unsplash.com/featured/800x600/?{keywords}`.
  - Overwrite `UrlToImage` with this refined image path on all newscards.
* **Double-Pass Deduplication**:
  - Always perform deduplication before and after cache-resolution on all feeds/pages to prevent any duplicate headlines or cover images.

## 2. Contextual Poll Generation
* For every refined article, generate a custom 4-option poll question related strictly to the article content.
* Save the poll directly in the `Polls` and `PollOptions` tables, storing the article URL in the `Subcategory` field to link them together.
* Expose and query the poll via `/api/polls/contextual?articleUrl={url}`.

## 3. GK Quiz Generation
* Generate a relevant GK trivia question (question, description, 4 options with a correct flag) from the article's details.
* Append the generated trivia question to `questions.json` in the project root.
* Merge the dynamic `questions.json` questions into the main quiz pool loaded in `QuizController.cs`.
