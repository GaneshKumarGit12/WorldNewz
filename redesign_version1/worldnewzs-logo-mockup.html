Keep the mark isolated

No other logos, rules, or text should sit inside the dashed boundary. This protects the "z" highlight from being read as a typo or a UI badge.

Do not

— Recolor the "z" to anything outside the red/gold pair defined in the token system
— Add a drop shadow, outline, or gradient fill
— Set the wordmark in a sans-serif substitute
— Stretch or condense the type
